## 🖥️ **IT Project Management Framework**

### 🔄 **Five Phases**
1. **Initiation** → Define objectives, feasibility, stakeholders.  
2. **Planning** → Scope, schedule, budget, risk register.  
3. **Execution** → Implement tasks, manage teams, deliver outputs.  
4. **Monitoring & Control** → Track KPIs, manage risks, ensure quality.  
5. **Closure** → Finalize deliverables, document lessons learned.  

---

### 👔 **Roles**
- **Project Manager** → Oversees planning & delivery.  
- **Business Analyst** → Translates business needs into technical requirements.  
- **Scrum Master** → Facilitates Agile processes.  
- **Product Owner** → Owns product vision & backlog.  
- **Development Team** → Builds and delivers IT solutions.  

---

### 📊 **Metrics**
- **Earned Value** → Cost & schedule performance.  
- **Critical Path** → Longest sequence of dependent tasks.  
- **ETC** → Forecast remaining cost.  
- **Risk Probability & Impact** → Prioritize IT risks.  
- **ROI** → Financial value vs cost.  
- **Resource Utilization** → Efficiency of IT staff & systems.  

---

✨ **Insight** —  
This framework highlights how IT projects move through **structured phases**, supported by **defined roles** and measured by **quantitative metrics**. It ensures projects are not only delivered but also aligned with business strategy and technical excellence.
