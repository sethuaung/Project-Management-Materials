The four consultant/architect roles in **Software Project Management** — with their **role, responsibilities, and skills** clearly described:  

---

## 🏗️ **Solutions Architect**  
- **Role:** Designs the **technical blueprint** of the solution, ensuring scalability, performance, and alignment with business goals.  
- **Responsibilities:**  
  - Define system architecture and integration points.  
  - Select appropriate technologies, frameworks, and platforms.  
  - Ensure compliance with security, scalability, and performance standards.  
  - Collaborate with stakeholders to align technical design with business needs.  
- **Skills:**  
  - Cloud computing (AWS, Azure, GCP).  
  - Enterprise architecture frameworks (TOGAF, Zachman).  
  - Microservices, APIs, DevOps practices.  
  - Strong communication and stakeholder management.  

---

## 💼 **Solutions Consultant**  
- **Role:** Acts as the **bridge between client needs and technical delivery**, focusing on solution fit and business value.  
- **Responsibilities:**  
  - Gather and analyze client requirements.  
  - Propose tailored solutions and demonstrate product capabilities.  
  - Support pre‑sales activities, workshops, and proof‑of‑concepts.  
  - Ensure solutions meet functional and business requirements.  
- **Skills:**  
  - Business analysis and requirement elicitation.  
  - ERP, CRM, BI tool knowledge.  
  - Strong presentation and demo skills.  
  - Customer engagement and relationship management.  

---

## ⚙️ **Technical Consultant**  
- **Role:** Provides **hands‑on technical expertise** in implementing and customizing solutions.  
- **Responsibilities:**  
  - Configure, customize, and integrate software systems.  
  - Troubleshoot technical issues and optimize performance.  
  - Provide technical training and documentation.  
  - Collaborate with developers and architects during delivery.  
- **Skills:**  
  - Programming (Java, Python, C#, etc.).  
  - Database management (SQL, NoSQL).  
  - System integration and API development.  
  - Problem‑solving and debugging.  

---

## 📊 **Functional Consultant**  
- **Role:** Focuses on **business processes and functional requirements**, ensuring the solution supports workflows effectively.  
- **Responsibilities:**  
  - Map business processes to system functionality.  
  - Define functional specifications and test cases.  
  - Conduct user training and support UAT (User Acceptance Testing).  
  - Act as liaison between business users and technical teams.  
- **Skills:**  
  - Deep domain expertise (finance, HR, supply chain, etc.).  
  - Requirement documentation (BRD, FRD, use cases).  
  - Testing and validation techniques.  
  - Strong communication and stakeholder collaboration.  

---

## 📊 **Quick Comparison Table**

| **Role** | **Focus** | **Responsibilities** | **Key Skills** |
|----------|-----------|-----------------------|----------------|
| **Solutions Architect** | Technical design | Define architecture, select tech, ensure scalability | Cloud, APIs, DevOps, frameworks |
| **Solutions Consultant** | Business fit | Gather requirements, demos, pre‑sales | Business analysis, ERP/CRM, client engagement |
| **Technical Consultant** | Implementation | Configure, customize, troubleshoot | Programming, databases, integration |
| **Functional Consultant** | Business processes | Map workflows, define specs, UAT | Domain expertise, documentation, testing |

---

✨ **Insight** —  
These roles complement each other in IT projects:  
- **Solutions Architect** → designs the *blueprint*.  
- **Solutions Consultant** → ensures the *solution fits business needs*.  
- **Technical Consultant** → builds and configures the *technical solution*.  
- **Functional Consultant** → validates the *business process alignment*.
