# Five major Project Management Methodologies

## 🧱 **Waterfall**

-   **Approach:** Sequential and linear — each phase must be completed before the next begins.
    
-   **Best For:** Projects with clear, fixed requirements and minimal expected changes.
    
-   **Strengths:** Predictable timeline, detailed documentation, easy progress tracking.
    
-   **Limitations:** Inflexible to change; late discovery of issues.
    

## 🔄 **Agile**

-   **Approach:** Iterative and adaptive — work delivered in short cycles (sprints).
    
-   **Best For:** Dynamic environments where requirements evolve.
    
-   **Strengths:** Flexibility, continuous feedback, faster delivery of value.
    
-   **Limitations:** Requires strong collaboration and disciplined teams.
    

## 🏃‍♂️ **Scrum**

-   **Approach:** Agile framework emphasizing teamwork, accountability, and iterative progress.
    
-   **Key Roles:** Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team.
    
-   **Strengths:** Clear structure, daily communication, rapid adaptation.
    
-   **Limitations:** Can be challenging for large or distributed teams.
    

## 📊 **Kanban**

-   **Approach:** Visual workflow management using boards and cards.
    
-   **Best For:** Continuous delivery and process optimization.
    
-   **Strengths:** Transparency, efficiency, easy prioritization.
    
-   **Limitations:** Less structured for complex projects requiring strict timelines.
    

## ⚙️ **Hybrid**

-   **Approach:** Combines Waterfall’s structure with Agile’s flexibility.
    
-   **Best For:** Organizations needing both predictability and adaptability.
    
-   **Strengths:** Balanced control and responsiveness; tailored to context.
    
-   **Limitations:** Requires careful integration of both methods.
    

## ✨ **Summary** 
— Each methodology offers unique advantages:

-   **Waterfall** ensures structure,
    
-   **Agile** drives adaptability,
    
-   **Scrum** fosters collaboration,
    
-   **Kanban** enhances visibility, and
    
-   **Hybrid** blends the best of both worlds.
