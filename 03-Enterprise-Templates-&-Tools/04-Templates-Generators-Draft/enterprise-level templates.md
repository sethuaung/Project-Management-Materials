## 2. Review Updated Scrum and Kanban Artifacts

Here are the brief overviews of the enhanced templates for the other two agile methodologies:

### Scrum Overview Template (Value Driven)

* **Focus:** Aligned with Google PM's emphasis on **value** and PMI's focus on **metrics**.
* **Key Addition:** Requires defining a **Target Velocity/Throughput** metric up front and explicitly listing the **Definition of Done (DoD)** as a mandatory **Quality Gate** for the increment.

### Kanban Overview Template (Flow Focused)

* **Focus:** Pure Kanban principles with a focus on flow and explicit policies, plus PMI metrics.
* **Key Addition:** Requires defining an explicit **Target Cycle Time** (a key PMI flow metric) and formalizing **Explicit Policies** (the rules for moving tasks across columns, minimizing ambiguity).

## 1. Scrum Overview Template (Value Driven)

This template is aligned with the principles of **Google Project Management** (focus on value, measurable goals) and incorporates essential **PMI metrics** and **Quality Gates**.

### Key Updates & Focus

| Section | PMBOK/IBM/Google Alignment | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Velocity** | **PMI Metric** | Quantifies the team's capacity (throughput) for formal tracking and forecasting. |
| **Project Vision & Value** | **Google PM Focus** | Ensures every effort is directly tied to a **Value Proposition** and answers the "Why?" (Business Case). |
| **Definition of Done (DoD)** | **Quality Gate** | Explicitly defines the quality and completion criteria required before the **Increment** can be accepted by the Product Owner (a crucial control point). |

## 2. Kanban Overview Template (Flow Focused)

This template formalizes the flow rules and metrics crucial for continuous delivery and optimization, often used by support, maintenance, or operations teams.

### Key Updates & Focus

| Section | PMBOK/IBM/Google Alignment | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Cycle Time** | **PMI/Kanban Metric** | Defines the time limit for an item to move from "Start" to "Done," enforcing efficiency. |
| **Explicit Policies** | **Kanban Principle** | Formalizes the "rules of the road" for moving tasks, which is critical for consistent quality and flow (aligned with IBM's focus on structured processes). |
| **WIP Limits** | **Core Kanban Control** | The mechanism for controlling the flow of work and avoiding bottlenecks. |
