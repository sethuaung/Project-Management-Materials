# 📚 Comprehensive Project Management Suite

### **Final Artifact Index**

This index catalogs every **core project management artifact**, organized by **methodology** and **phase** (Initiation, Planning, Execution, Control). It ensures complete documentation coverage across **Scrum, Kanban, Waterfall, and PRINCE2**.

## I. **Methodology Overviews & Initiation**

|Artifact Title  | Filepath |Purpose & Alignment |
|--|--|--|
| Scrum Overview Template | `scrum_overview_template.md` | Defines value-driven process, roles, events, Definition of Done (DoD), and Target Velocity KPI. |
| Kanban Overview Template|`kanban_overview_template.md` |Defines flow process, WIP Limits, Explicit Policies, and tracks Target Cycle Time KPI. |
|PRINCE2 Project Initiation Document (PID) |`project_initiation_document.md` | Foundational document including Business Case, Quality Criteria, and formal Scope.|

|Artifact Title  | Filepath |Purpose & Key Deliverable |
|--|--|--|
| Requirements Specification| `waterfall_requirements_template.md`| Defines scope, Functional (FR) & Non-Functional (NFR) requirements, with stakeholder sign-off.
|
|High-Level Design Document |`waterfall_design_document.md` | Technical blueprint: architecture, stack, security, and deployment design.|
|Gantt Chart Template | `waterfall_gantt_chart_template.md`| Schedule baseline with dependencies, duration estimates, and milestones.|
| Acceptance Test Plan|`waterfall_test_plan_template.md` | QA strategy, defect management, and strict Entry/Exit Criteria (Quality Gates).|

| Artifact Title | Filepath | Purpose & Key Metrics |
