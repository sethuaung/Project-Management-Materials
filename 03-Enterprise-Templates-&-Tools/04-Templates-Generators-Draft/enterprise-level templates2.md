1.  **Financial and Schedule Performance Tracking:** A simple way to see if you are on budget and on time.
2.  **Resource Management:** A basic way to assign and monitor who is doing what.

2.  **Resource Allocation Matrix:** A simple tool to map team members to project tasks and time commitments.
