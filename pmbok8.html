1. Introduction & Fundamentals

PMBOK 8 transitions PMI's standard from rigid process groups to principle-based performance domains while reintroducing 40 explicit processes suited for any delivery approach. A project is defined as a temporary endeavor creating a unique deliverable under scope, schedule, and cost constraints.

Key Focus: Life cycle models, early governance via the Project Canvas tool, PMO structures, and strategic alignment through Portfolio and Program management.

6. Delivery Models & Process Changes

Agile Integration

Agile practices (backlog refinement, sprints, retrospectives) are natively embedded directly into standard process domains rather than treated as external add-ons.

Methodology Choice

Select Predictive for fixed constraints, Agile for high uncertainty/innovation, or Hybrid to combine governance with rapid iteration.

Process Consolidation

Consolidated Lead the Team (Develop + Manage Team) and Perform Risk Analysis (Qual + Quant Analysis) into single streamlined processes.
